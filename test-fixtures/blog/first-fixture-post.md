## What the price held

An option bought the day before a report holds a guess about how far the stock will move.

- The stock opened up 4%.
- The call lost 21% of its value.

> The guess was spent when the report came out.

## What each number counts

| Number | What it counts |
|---|---|
| Stock move | The change in the share price |
| Call change | The change in the option price |
