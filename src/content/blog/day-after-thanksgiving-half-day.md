The day after Thanksgiving is one of the two quietest trading days in most years, because the market is open for only half of it. On average, hour for hour, it trades about as many shares as a normal day. This year the session runs from 9:30 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. New York time, on Friday 27 November.

## 1. The facts

- **The session:** 3½ hours, from 9:30 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. A normal day runs to 4:00 p.m., which is 6½ hours.
- **The options:** most stop trading at 1:00 p.m. The few that normally trade until 4:15 stop at 1:15.
- **The gap before it:** the regular session ends at 4:00 p.m. on Wednesday and starts again at 9:30 a.m. on Friday. That is 41½ hours, against 17½ on a normal night.
- **The record:** from 2015 to 2025 it was the year's quietest day by shares traded five times, and the second quietest three times.

## 2. Half the hours, half the shares

The table counts every share traded in US stocks that day, on the exchanges and on the off-exchange venues that report to FINRA. A normal day is the median day of that year by shares traded. The rank is among all of that year's trading days, quietest first.

| Year | Shares traded | Of a normal day | Rank |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2015 | 2.86 billion | 42% | 2nd |
| 2016 | 3.01 billion | 42% | 1st |
| 2017 | 2.78 billion | 43% | 1st |
| 2018 | 3.42 billion | 49% | 1st |
| 2019 | 3.66 billion | 53% | 2nd |
| 2020 | 7.03 billion | 67% | 8th |
| 2021 | 9.08 billion | 85% | 32nd |
| 2022 | 4.56 billion | 40% | 1st |
| 2023 | 5.05 billion | 47% | 1st |
| 2024 | 8.48 billion | 73% | 3rd |
| 2025 | 9.25 billion | 54% | 2nd |

The average of the eleven years is 54% of a normal day. A session of 3½ hours is 54% of a normal one, to the nearest point. Single years spread widely around that, from 40% in 2022 to 85% in 2021, the year the half day had news to trade.

## 3. 2021, when the news broke the day before

South Africa reported a new coronavirus variant to the World Health Organization on Wednesday 24 November 2021, the day before Thanksgiving. The US market was shut on the Thursday. The WHO named the variant Omicron on Friday 26 November.

The half day traded 9.08 billion shares. The full Wednesday before it had traded 9.10 billion. By dollar value the half day traded $565 billion, against $516 billion on the Wednesday and $536 billion on the median day of 2021. The S&P 500 fell 2.3% from Wednesday's close, its third-worst daily fall of 2021.

## 4. What the short day changes

On Friday 27 November the day's closing prices are set at 1:00 p.m. Options expiring that Friday stop trading three hours earlier than on a normal Friday. The holder of an expiring stock option normally has until 5:30 p.m. to decide whether to exercise it, 90 minutes after the close. [Our post on expiration's last 90 minutes](/blog/expiring-option-after-the-close) shows what can happen in that window. When the close is moved, FINRA's rule moves the deadline with it, to 90 minutes after the new close. This Friday that is 2:30 p.m.
